In this we learn about how encoder and decoder architecture work, we also talk about the integration of attention mechanism
We'll cover both the attention mechanism techniques, which includes bhadnau and Luong attention.

initally discovered for NMT (neural machine translation) task. goal is to convert sentences from one language to another.
2 major problems in previous architecture is:
    1. order in which word occurs matters
    2. arbitary length input and outputs.

because of above 2 problems we choose encoder-decoder with RNNs.

execution pipeline:
    raw_sentences  ==> tokenized sentences ==> create_vocabulary ==> numericalization ==> padding ==> batching ==> encoder training
