UNET is deep learning model used for image semantic segmentation task in image analysis.
Architecture:
    It is consist of 2 parts: Encoder and Decoder.

Encoder: Series of convolution and pooling layer that progressively downsample the input image to extract features at multiple scale.

Decoder: It consist of series of convolution and upsampling layers that upsamples the feature map to original input image size.
